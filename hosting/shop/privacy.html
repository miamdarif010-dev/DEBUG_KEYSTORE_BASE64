Privacy Policy

গোপনীয়তা নীতি

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Contact: miamdarif1234@gmail.com

1. Who we are

BuyNova (“we”, “us”) is a shopping and reselling app where sellers list products, entrepreneurs (resellers) create their own stores, and customers place orders. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

BuyNova একটি শপিং ও রিসেলিং অ্যাপ। এই পেজে বলা আছে আমরা কী তথ্য নিই, কেন নিই এবং আপনার কী অধিকার আছে।

2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the app:

  • Account: name, email address, phone number, profile photo, and your sign-in method (email & password or Google). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password.
  • Orders and delivery: delivery name, phone, address, city, district, items bought, prices, order status and tracking.
  • Wallet and rewards: wallet balance and transactions, reward points, referral and invite activity.
  • Content you upload: product photos and videos, store details, reviews, and messages you submit to us.
  • Notifications: a push-notification token for your device, and your notification choice.
  • Device and ads: advertising ID and basic device and usage data used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads.
  • Email codes: when you register with email, we send a 6-digit verification code to your email address.

অ্যাপ চালাতে যতটুকু দরকার, ততটুকুই নিই।

3. How we use information

We use your information to:

  • create and secure your account and verify your email;
  • process orders, deliveries, returns, refunds and wallet payments;
  • show products, stores and notifications (for example new products and app updates);
  • prevent fraud and abuse, including fake accounts and reward farming;
  • show ads, and let you earn reward points for watching rewarded ads;
  • provide support and improve the app.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed:

  • Sellers: the seller of an item you order receives your name, phone and delivery address so that the order can be delivered. Resellers see order status and totals for orders from their store.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions, Storage, Cloud Messaging) and Cloudinary (image and video hosting), and Google AdMob (ads). They process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Email delivery: verification codes are sent through an email service.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law or to protect users and our rights.

আমরা আপনার তথ্য বিক্রি করি না। শুধু প্রয়োজন হলে নিচের পক্ষের সাথে শেয়ার করি।

5. Ads

We show ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID to show and measure ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings (Google → Ads).

বিজ্ঞাপন দেখানোর জন্য Google AdMob ব্যবহার করা হয়। ফোনের Settings থেকে আপনি Advertising ID রিসেট বা সীমিত করতে পারেন।

6. Permissions

The app may ask for access to your photos, camera or videos only when you choose to upload a picture or video, and for notifications so we can send you updates. You can change these in your phone’s settings at any time.

ছবি, ক্যামেরা ও নোটিফিকেশনের অনুমতি শুধু আপনি আপলোড করলে বা আপডেট পেতে চাইলে চাওয়া হয়। ফোনের Settings থেকে যেকোনো সময় বদলাতে পারবেন।

7. Notifications

You can turn notifications off in the app (Settings → Notifications) or in your phone settings.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time: in the app go to Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account, or see how to delete your account. When you delete your account we delete your profile and personal data. We may keep limited records of orders, payments and wallet transactions where required by law or to prevent fraud and resolve disputes.

অ্যাপের Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account থেকে যেকোনো সময় অ্যাকাউন্ট মুছতে পারবেন। আইন বা প্রতারণা প্রতিরোধের জন্য অর্ডার ও লেনদেনের সীমিত রেকর্ড রাখা হতে পারে।

9. Security

We use Firebase security rules, server-side checks for orders and wallet balances, and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

10. Children

BuyNova is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your choices

You can access and update your name, email and phone in the app, turn notifications off, delete your account, or contact us to ask about the data we hold.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email miamdarif1234@gmail.com.
