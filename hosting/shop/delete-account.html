Delete your BuyNova account

You can delete your account and personal data yourself inside the app.

অ্যাপের ভেতর থেকেই নিজের অ্যাকাউন্ট ও তথ্য মুছে ফেলতে পারবেন।

Steps in the app

  1. Open BuyNova and log in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm, then enter your password (or sign in with Google again) when asked.

ধাপ: অ্যাপ খুলুন → Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account → নিশ্চিত করুন ও পাসওয়ার্ড দিন।

Cannot open the app?

Email miamdarif1234@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify and delete the account.

What is deleted

Your login account and profile data (name, email, phone, photo, saved addresses), notification tokens, and your wallet and reward balance are removed.

What may be kept

Limited records of past orders, payments and wallet transactions may be kept where required by law or to prevent fraud and resolve disputes. Wallet money that is still in your balance should be withdrawn before deleting the account.
